Visiting & Booking

We're open Tuesday through Sunday, 9:00 AM to 7:30 PM. We're closed on Mondays for deep cleaning. Holiday hours may vary — check our social media for updates.
Walk-ins are welcome subject to capacity. However, we recommend booking online to guarantee your spot. Members get 14-day advance booking priority. Same-day reservations close 1 hour before the slot starts.
Free cancellation up to 4 hours before your reserved slot. Within 4 hours, a $5 per child fee applies. For party bookings, free reschedule up to 7 days before the event.
Street parking is available on Vanderbilt Ave and surrounding streets. There's also a municipal lot on Dean Street, a 3-minute walk away. We're a 5-minute walk from the Bergen St. F/G station.

Safety & Health

We are a nut-free studio. We ask about allergies at check-in, label every snack, and separate preparation surfaces. For parties, we work with allergy-aware catering partners. Please inform us of any allergies when booking.
If your child has had a fever, vomiting, or any contagious symptoms in the last 24 hours, please reschedule — no fee, no questions asked. This policy protects every family in the studio.
Every team member holds current CPR and first-aid certification. Our floor hosts complete a 40-hour training program covering child development, allergy response, de-escalation techniques, and emergency protocols.
High-touch surfaces, balls, and mats are cleaned hourly with a hospital-grade botanical disinfectant. The entire studio receives a deep clean every night with industrial equipment.

Membership & Pricing

No contracts. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. Your membership runs until the end of the current billing period.

Visit passes are per-child and can be shared between siblings within the same household. Memberships can also include guest passes — check your plan for details.
