Our story

Born in Brooklyn, built for kids.

Whirl & Wonder began in 2019 when two Park Slope parents — a pediatric occupational therapist and a product designer — realized there was no indoor play space in Brooklyn that took both safety and developmental science seriously.

They spent 18 months researching play environments across Scandinavia, Japan, and Australia before opening the first studio on Vanderbilt Avenue. Every detail — from the foam density in The Cocoon to the sightlines in The Skyfort — was designed with input from child development experts.

Today, we serve over 2,000 Brooklyn families with age-graded play zones, sensory programs, holiday camps, and the kind of birthday parties that kids actually remember.

Our mission

What we believe.

We believe play is not a break from learning — it is the learning. Our mission is to provide the safest, most thoughtfully designed indoor play environments for children ages 0–12 in Brooklyn.

Safety First

ASTM F1918 certified equipment, hourly sanitization, CPR-trained staff, and wristband check-in. Every protocol exists because a parent asked "what if?"

Play-Led Growth

Every zone is built around a developmental window. We don't direct play — we facilitate the conditions for children to direct their own discovery.

Community Roots

We're a Brooklyn business, for Brooklyn families. We partner with local vendors, hire from the neighborhood, and donate 2% of revenue to NYC children's charities.
