Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Whirl & Wonder (our website and physical studio), we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing information when you make a reservation, purchase a pass, or sign up for a membership.
  • Child Information: Name, age, and any medical or allergy information you voluntarily provide for safety purposes.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns.
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies to maintain your session and preference settings. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • To process reservations, memberships, and party bookings
  • To communicate with you about your bookings, account, and studio updates
  • To ensure the safety and well-being of children in our care
  • To improve our services and website experience
  • To send optional newsletter updates (only if you subscribe)

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • As required by law or legal process
  • With service providers who assist in our operations (e.g., payment processors), bound by strict confidentiality agreements

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS), secure server infrastructure, and access controls. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. We collect child-related information only for safety and operational purposes (e.g., allergy alerts, emergency contacts). This information is stored securely and is never shared with third parties. Parents/guardians may request deletion of their child's information at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. After account deletion, we retain data for up to 30 days before permanent removal, except where required by law.

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
