03 — Safety

The safety details,
taken seriously.

The fun only works if the trust does. Here's exactly how we keep kids safe, what we clean, who watches the room, and how we handle allergies, illness, and emergencies.

Inspected quarterly ASTM F1918 indoor play equipment standard
CPR & first-aid certified Every host, every shift
01

Sanitized, hourly

High-touch surfaces, balls, and mats cleaned with a hospital-grade botanical disinfectant. Full deep clean nightly.

02

Visible, hands-on staff

Floor hosts wear high-vis aprons. We don't hide behind a desk — we're in the play space, every minute.

03

Strict illness policy

Fever, vomiting, or contagious symptoms in the last 24h? Please reschedule — no fee, no questions.

04

Allergy-aware by default

Nut-free studio. We label every snack, separate prep surfaces, and ask about allergies at check-in.

05

Wristband check-in

Each child gets a numbered wristband; only matched adults can sign them out. Photo ID required.

06

Open sightlines

No hidden corners. Every zone visible from at least two staff positions at all times.
